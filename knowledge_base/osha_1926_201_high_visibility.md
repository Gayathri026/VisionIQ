# OSHA Standard 1926.201: Signaling and High-Visibility Apparel

## Section 1: Scope and Retroreflective Performance
Flaggers and workers exposed to public traffic or construction machinery shall wear high-visibility safety apparel meeting the requirements of ISEA/ANSI 107-2015, "American National Standard for High-Visibility Safety Apparel and Accessories":
- Class 2 garments are required for daytime or twilight operations where machinery speeds exceed 25 mph.
- Class 3 garments are mandatory for nighttime operations or complex background visual clutter.
- Retroreflective material shall be visible at a minimum distance of 1,000 feet in all directions.

## Section 2: Torso Containment and Spatial Visibility
Safety vests and shirts must cover the central torso region (minimum 201 square inches of background fluorescent material for Class 2). Obscured, soiled, or unfastened vests that fail to achieve 360-degree retroreflectivity are non-compliant and create severe collision hazards around mobile industrial excavators and transport haulers.

## Section 3: Recommended Corrective Action
Upon visual confirmation of absent or obstructed high-visibility apparel in active transit corridors:
1. Re-position worker out of active vehicle pathways immediately.
2. Provide ISEA 107 certified fluorescent lime-yellow or orange-red vest.
3. Conduct pre-shift inspection of reflective banding for grease contamination.
