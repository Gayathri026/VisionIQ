# ISO 21920 / ISO 4287: Geometrical Product Specifications — Surface Texture

## Section 1: Distinction between Roughness and Discrete Defects
Standard surface engineering specifications establish critical distinctions between continuous surface texture parameters and discrete metallurgical defects:
- Profile Roughness Parameters (Ra, Rz, Rmax): Arithmetic mean deviation of the assessed profile resulting from machining tools, grinding grits, or rolling mill roll surface roughness.
- Waviness (Wa, Wt): Wider spacing irregularities resulting from machine tool vibration, chatter, or workpiece deflection.
- Discrete Surface Anomalies: Localized fractures, pits, inclusions, or gouges that breach the statistical envelope of nominal roughness.

## Section 2: Visual Inspection Limitations
Two-dimensional optical imaging cannot reliably measure depth or distinguish between minor reflective striations (normal Ra = 0.8 um) and rejectable mechanical gouges without calibrated tactile profilometry or confocal laser scanning. Computer vision systems must report surface variations as unconfirmed anomaly cues pending tactile or optical interferometry verification.
