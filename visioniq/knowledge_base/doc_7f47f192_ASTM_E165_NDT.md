# ASTM E165 Non-Destructive Testing Protocol

Section 4.1 Liquid Penetrant Examination:
Liquid penetrant examination is a sensitive method of detecting surface-breaking defects such as hairline cracks, crazing networks, and porosity in ferrous and non-ferrous metals.

Section 4.2 Minimum Dwell Time:
The standard penetrant dwell time for stainless and carbon sheet steels shall be at least 15 minutes.
