# ASTM E155: Standard Reference Radiographs and Non-Destructive Examination

## Section 1: Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Protocol Hierarchy
When automated computer vision detects visual surface anomalies or localized color deviations on structural components, destructive metallographic analysis must not be immediately assumed. A non-destructive evaluation sequence must be executed:
1. Visual and Optical Microscopy (VT): Magnification at 10x-50x to confirm crack open-mouth width.
2. Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT per ASTM E165): Applicable to non-porous metals to highlight micro-fissures and hairline crazing.
3. Magnetic Particle Testing (MT per ASTM E1444): Applicable to ferromagnetic steels for surface and near-surface linear indications.
4. Ultrasonic Testing (UT per ASTM E114): High-frequency acoustic wave reflection to verify subsurface depth and internal delamination.
5. Radiographic Examination (RT per ASTM E155 / ASTM E94): Volumetric evaluation against reference radiographs to classify gas porosity, dross inclusions, and shrinkage cavities.

## Section 2: Disposition and Root-Cause Isolation
Visual anomalies detected by computer vision must be classified as preliminary indications until verified by qualified Level II/III NDT personnel. Surface discoloration from protective rolling oil or water stains must not be dispositioned as metallurgical cracking without solvent wipe and penetrant confirmation.
