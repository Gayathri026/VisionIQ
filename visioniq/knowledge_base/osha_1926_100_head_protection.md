# OSHA Standard 1926.100: Head Protection and Hard Hats in Construction

## Section 1: Scope and Applicability
Employees working in areas where there is a possible danger of head injury from impact, or from falling or flying objects, or from electrical shock and burns, shall be protected by protective helmets and hard hats conforming strictly to the American National Standards Institute (ANSI) specifications.

## Section 2: Technical Specifications and ANSI Standards
Protective helmets and hard hats for the protection of employees against impact and penetration of falling and flying objects shall meet the specifications contained in American National Standards Institute (ANSI) Z89.1-2014 / ISEA Z89.1-2014, "American National Standard for Industrial Head Protection":
- Type I helmets / hard hats are intended to reduce the force of impact resulting from a blow only to the top of the head.
- Type II helmets / hard hats are intended to reduce the force of lateral impact resulting from an off-center, lateral, or top blow.
- Class E (Electrical) helmets are tested to withstand 20,000 volts.
- Class G (General) helmets are tested to withstand 2,200 volts.
- Class C (Conductive) helmets provide zero electrical insulation protection.

## Section 3: Enforcement and Corrective Actions
Where visual inspection indicates a worker in an active crane, scaffolding, or heavy machinery hazard zone without head protection or hard hat:
1. Immediate stop-work directive must be issued for that individual.
2. Verify ANSI Z89.1 compliance stamp embossed inside shell.
3. Inspect suspension harness for UV degradation or structural stress cracks.
4. Record formal safety audit entry prior to site re-entry.
