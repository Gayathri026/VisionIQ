# Model Card: VisionIQ PPE & Safety Compliance Detector

## Model Details
- **Model Name:** VisionIQ PPE & Safety Compliance Detector (`visioniq-ppe-yolo11n-v1`)
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Date:** October 2026
- **Model Type:** Real-Time Anchor-Free Deep Object Detector
- **Architecture:** Ultralytics YOLO11n (C3k2 feature extractor, SPPF, C2PSA attention, decoupled anchor-free detection heads)
- **Parameters:** 2,591,790 (~2.6M)
- **FLOPs:** 6.4 GFLOPs @ $640 \times 640$
- **Target Platform:** Edge Industrial IPCs, Jetson Orin Nano, and Cloud CPU/GPU nodes
- **Framework:** PyTorch 2.4.1+, Ultralytics 8.3.0
- **License:** CC BY 4.0

## Intended Use
- **Primary Use Case:** Real-time visual monitoring of personal protective equipment (PPE) adherence on construction, manufacturing, and heavy infrastructure work zones.
- **Recognized Target Classes (10 classes):**
  1. `Hardhat`
  2. `Mask`
  3. `NO-Hardhat` (Safety Violation)
  4. `NO-Mask` (Safety Violation)
  5. `NO-Safety Vest` (Safety Violation)
  6. `Person`
  7. `Safety Cone`
  8. `Safety Vest`
  9. `machinery`
  10. `vehicle`
- **Out-of-Scope Use Cases:** Individual facial recognition, biometric surveillance, punitive worker tracking, or operating outside industrial safety regulatory frameworks.

## Training Data & Methodology
- **Dataset:** Roboflow Construction Site Safety Image Dataset (v30)
- **Source Link:** `https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/snehilsanyal/construction-site-safety-image-dataset-roboflow`
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Split Strategy:** Grouped by capture session / camera vantage to eliminate spatial-temporal test leakage.
- **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer with cosine learning rate schedule ($\eta_{\text{base}} = 0.001$, momentum $\beta_1 = 0.9$, weight decay $\lambda = 5 \times 10^{-4}$).
- **Loss Functions:** Complete IoU (CIoU) / Distribution Focal Loss (DFL) for box regression, Binary Cross-Entropy with logit loss for multi-label class prediction.
- **Reproducibility:** Deterministic random seed fixed to $42$ across Python `random`, NumPy, and PyTorch backends.

## Evaluation Metrics & Expected Performance
- **Primary Metrics:**
  - Precision ($P$)
  - Recall ($R$)
  - $\text{mAP}@0.5$ (Mean Average Precision at IoU threshold 0.50)
  - $\text{mAP}@[0.5:0.95]$ (COCO standard primary metric across thresholds 0.50 to 0.95)
- **Inference Latency:** $\sim 126.4\text{ ms}$ on 6-core x86 CPU, $\sim 9.8\text{ ms}$ on NVIDIA RTX 3060/4060.
- **Safety Violation Resolution:** High sensitivity towards negative violation classes (`NO-Hardhat`, `NO-Safety Vest`) to prevent false-negative alarms in high-risk zones.

## Ethical Considerations & Operational Limitations
- **Occlusion & Adverse Lighting:** Detection precision degrades in severe dust storms, heavy night rain, or extreme distance from overhead cranes where helmet diameter is $< 10\text{ px}$.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Alerts generated by `visioniq-ppe-yolo11n-v1` serve as decision support for Safety Officers and Site Supervisors and must not trigger automated punitive penalties.
